## 1. Method

**How I viewed the boards**
- **Overview:** screenshot of the canvas at the pane's native size (1276×1146 CSS px, returned downscaled to 800px wide).
- **Full-window view (worked):**
  - PLAY → "Play full screen" on Main and Peek.
  - The ⤢ expand control on the other boards.
  - The canvas ▷ menu, which lists every board. This is the only way I found into Title bars, whose name strip has no ⤢.
  - Each opens a `#play-<id>` view with a **Fill | Fit** toggle. Fluid boards were judged in **Fill**, where the board takes the viewport width; fixed boards in **Fit**, at roughly 1:1 / 0.53.
- **Not used:** I did not try double-clicking boards or name strips, because a double-click risked starting a rename or edit.

**Widths tested**
- **Every PAGE board:** 1276 (native), 1440×900, 1280×800, 1024×768, 768×1024 ("tablet") and 390×844 (custom). The "mobile" preset is 375×812, so I ran 390×844 to match the brief and used 375 once, on Main.
- **Extra widths hunting break points:**
  - Main: 1130, 1140, 1300, 1320, 1340, 1360, 1380, 1420
  - Peek: 1520, 1600
  - Stress: 1360, 1395, 830, 810
- **Exception:** Empty was not shot at 1280. It's bracketed by 1440 and 1024 and is a single centered max-width card.

**What I could not do, and why**
- **No DOM access inside boards.** Each board is a cross-origin iframe (`*.frame.claudeusercontent.com`), so `read_page`, `get_page_text` and JS can't see inside it. All text and structure claims come from screenshots plus the `.dc.html` source.
- **Limited pixel precision at desktop widths.** Viewports wider than ~800 come back downscaled (0.55–0.8), and `zoom` only crops; it never magnifies. Exact px values at 1024+ come from the source. Fine alignment was judged at 1:1 at 768 and 390.
- **No board prop changes.** Changing props would write to the canvas. So I did not view:
  - the other theme of any board (each board shows only its default theme);
  - Peek `back` (←);
  - Find `goto`;
  - Settings `parseError`;
  - Empty `dragging` and `firstRun`;
  - Main `density`.

  Those are judged from source only.
- **Main/Peek lens toggles** (Rendered/Raw, Grid/Raw in play mode): not exercised.
- **Harness artifacts:**
  - The play view eats ~80px of chrome, so a 1440×900 viewport gives the board ~1425×820.
  - Screenshots taken in the same batch as a resize were stale: the iframe hadn't re-laid out. I discarded them and re-shot after a 1–2s wait.
- **Spec range:** the spec's minimum window is 720×480 (§9). The 390 results are beyond the spec, reported because the brief asked for them. 768 and up are in range.

**Read-only check**
- **No canvas content touched.** I made no drags, typing, prop, comment or style changes.
- **What I did touch:**
  - Main got selected (selection handles) when returning from play.
  - Main's play menu was triggered twice.
  - The canvas zoom menu was opened and closed with Esc.
  - The play-view Fill/Fit toggle was clicked twice and left on Fill for Markdown. Other fixed boards opened in Fit by default.
- **Evidence:** at the end of the session, the canvas zoom menu still shows **Undo and Redo greyed out**, so no edit is in the history.

## 2. Ranked findings

### BLOCKER 1: the multi-card layout wraps and stacks instead of splitting (systemic)
- **Where:** Main, Flush, Tree, Problems, Stress, plus Peek (card collapse).
- **What I saw:**
  - **Two-card boards stack.**
    - Cards are `flex:1 1 420px` in a `flex-wrap:wrap` row (Main.dc.html:78/80/142; Flush.dc.html:45/46/85; Tree.dc.html:44/45/79).
    - They stack vertically below **~1135px**: stacked at 1130, side-by-side at 1140.
    - At 1024 the csv card sits entirely below the fold [blob-1792517712379-pcnznn, blob-1792517718140-zg6yvb]. Flush at 1024 shows ~4 csv rows [blob-1792517999003-73mcga]. Tree stacks at 1024 [blob-1792518201751-3sszkk].
  - **Three-card boards wrap.**
    - Stress is 3-up only above **~1386px**: 2+1 at 1360, 3-up at 1395. So at **1280 it's 2+1** [blob-1792518394955-kw3g54, blob-1792518682190-5zupj3, blob-1792518682190-bu5i9q].
    - Problems goes 3→2+1 below ~1127, and the whole-file-invalid card drops below the fold at 1024 [blob-1792518347302-bdvoo9].
  - **Cards with an `position:absolute; inset:0` body collapse when wrapped.**
    - This is the case in Stress.dc.html:49 and Peek.dc.html:64.
    - **Stress at 830 and 768:** the peek card has **zero height**; only its pill remains [blob-1792518417403-wup8bj, blob-1792518690260-zh7mj0].
    - **Stress at 1024:** squashed to ~130px [blob-1792518417403-f8995c].
    - **Peek at 768:** ~280px [blob-1792517819751-ectx8z].
- **Why it matters:**
  - The spec has **left/right splits only**; top/bottom was removed (§0.3, §3.7).
  - Cards shrink to a **320px minimum**, and a split that would go below that opens as a tab instead (§3.5).
  - §10.12 literally specifies "three cards at 1280px wide". The board fails at the named width.
  - The mock teaches the implementer a web-reflow behaviour the product must not have.
- **Fix:**
  - Use a **non-wrapping** card row with each card `flex:1 1 0; min-width:320px`. In the mock, show overflow as the "Not enough room to split" toast state rather than wrapping.
  - Derive the gutter from card count: `gap: 16px` at ≤2 cards, `8px` at ≥3. Problems keeps 8px when it wraps to 2 per row.
  - Card bodies must be in normal flow (`display:flex; flex-direction:column; min-height:0`), never `position:absolute; inset:0`, so a card can't collapse to 0.

### BLOCKER 2: the Grid lens squeezes fractional columns instead of sizing and scrolling (systemic)
- **Where:** Main, Flush, Peek, Find (390), and every narrow csv card.
- **What I saw:**
  - Columns are `minmax(0, N fr)` with a fixed 64px `est_hours` column and `overflow:hidden` on the grid (Main.dc.html:165/174; Peek.dc.html:106/114; Find.dc.html grid rows).
  - **"To do" status pills wrap onto two lines** below **~1330px**: wrapping at 1320, single line at 1340 [blob-1792517696058-t7sf4n, blob-1792518643117-q72urr, blob-1792518643117-x8yf4l, blob-1792518658442-xi9kr8].
  - **Header "due" collides with "# est_hours"** ("due# est_hours") up to **~1420px**; at 1440 the two labels are touching [blob-1792518651177-i0321h, blob-1792518651177-izl9v3]. The "#" label in a 64px column overflows left into `due`.
  - **Owner names are truncated** ("Maya C…") while `task` has slack.
  - **Status column clipped by the card edge** ("Doin", header "stati"):
    - at 1140, just before the cards stack [blob-1792518643117-a6eh9t];
    - at 390 in Main, Flush and Find [blob-1792517754371-448q0m, blob-1792518503084-7f1n76].
  - **No horizontal scroll anywhere.** The only exception is Stress's fake thumb on a fixed 1080px inner grid.
- **Why it matters:**
  - Spec §5.3: "columns auto-fit from the first 1,000 rows, capped at 360px", sticky header and row-number column, virtualized horizontal scroll.
  - The hero screen is broken at 1280, the most common laptop width.
  - Value pills splitting mid-word undermine "values are verbatim" visually.
- **Fix:**
  - Columns: `grid-template-columns: 48px repeat(n, minmax(min(<fitted>, 360px), max-content))`, with the grid in an `overflow-x:auto` scroller. Header row and row-number column `position:sticky`.
  - Pills `white-space:nowrap`.
  - Header labels `white-space:nowrap; overflow:hidden; text-overflow:ellipsis`, with the type glyph `flex:none`.
  - Truncate a column only at its 360px cap, with a tooltip.

### BLOCKER 3: the peek never shows its own title at or below the 1440 design size
- **Where:** Peek board, plus the Stress peek variant.
- **What I saw:**
  - **Source:** peek is `width:45%; min-width:420px`, no `max-width` (Peek.dc.html:88). The header always carries text buttons "Open as tab" and "Open to side" (Peek.dc.html:101–102).
  - **1440:** breadcrumb "… / tasks.cs", hard-clipped against the lens switch [blob-1792517808126-xsb34q].
  - **1276 / 1280:** a bare "…"; the filename is gone [blob-1792517801600-20h7wa, blob-1792517812948-qzwiw6].
  - **1024:** nothing [blob-1792517816377-4u3484].
  - **Full filename:** visible only from **somewhere between 1440 and 1520** [blob-1792518670399-79hbua].
  - **390:** the 420px floor exceeds the ~350px card, so the peek **overflows off the left edge of the window** (icon, "Grid", row numbers cut) [blob-1792517825186-x0vzt1].
- **Why it matters:**
  - The peek is the signature behaviour (Z2/Z10), and it doesn't say what file it's showing.
  - Spec §4.5:
    - width `max(45%, 420px)` **clamped to the window**;
    - "below 520px of peek width, the actions become icons with tooltips";
    - the header carries the breadcrumb.
  - The ← variant (`back` prop) costs another 34px, from source.
- **Fix:**
  - `width: max(45%, 420px); max-width: calc(100vw - 32px)`, anchored right, never past the window's left edge.
  - A container rule at `<520px` collapses the two actions to 28px icon buttons with tooltips. Stress already draws them (Stress.dc.html:55+).
  - Breadcrumb filename: `flex:0 1 auto; min-width:10ch; text-overflow:ellipsis`; root/middle segments shrink first (`flex-shrink:10`).

### BLOCKER 4: the pill-overflow ⌄ disappears exactly when the row overflows
- **Where:** Stress.
- **What I saw:**
  - The "6 ⌄" all-tabs button is the last flex item inside a tablist with `overflow:hidden` (Stress.dc.html:110–116).
  - **1440:** the README card is ~370px, the pills overflow, and the ⌄ is pushed out and clipped [blob-1792518394954-bgs4jo].
  - **1024 and 768:** same; "schem" is cut and there's no ⌄ [blob-1792518417403-f8995c, blob-1792518417403-wup8bj].
  - **1276 / 1280:** the ⌄ only appears here, where the card wrapped to full width and nothing overflows [blob-1792518378929-jz681p].
- **Why it matters:** §3.6 and §10.12 call for the ⌄ that "lists every tab in that card". It's only visible when it's useless.
- **Fix:**
  - Split the row into `[scroller: flex:1; min-width:0; overflow-x:auto; padding-inline:2px] [⌄: flex:none]`.
  - The ⌄ lives outside the clipped region and is always visible when `scrollWidth > clientWidth`.

### MAJOR 1: PAGE boards only adapt to width; they don't fill or fit the window height (systemic, all 14 PAGE boards)
- **What I saw:**
  - **Source:** every root is `min-height:900px; overflow:hidden` (e.g. Main.dc.html:20), and `body` has no background.
  - **Taller window:** a **white band** shows under the dark UI (1276 native) [blob-1792517656582-ora0ac, blob-1792517801600-20h7wa].
  - **Shorter window (1440×900 viewport ≈ 820 tall, 1280×800, 1024×768):** the board scrolls, so card bottoms, the Outline pill and peek bottoms sit off-screen [blob-1792517681921-zbipvs].
  - **Markdown in Fill:** the card body clips its own content. The task list and the **filled code block that §10.5 requires** only appear in Fit mode [blob-1792517896258-ugop09 vs blob-1792517904485-eyo6s5].
- **Why it matters:**
  - Vertical behaviour can't be judged at all.
  - A white flash under a dark app is exactly the bug a reviewer will copy into the build.
- **Fix:**
  - Root `height:100vh; min-height:480px` (the spec's minimum window).
  - `html, body { margin:0; background: var(--canvas) }`.
  - Card bodies `overflow:auto` rather than `hidden`.

### MAJOR 2: below ~815px the sidebar "stacks", which isn't in the spec and wastes the screen
- **What I saw:**
  - The sidebar is `flex:0 0 240px` and main is `flex:999 1 560px` in a wrapping row. It wraps between 810 and 830 [blob-1792518690260-zh7mj0, blob-1792518690261-tsom1s].
  - **Main at 768:** the stacked sidebar keeps 240px, leaving a **~510×340px dead zone** to its right [blob-1792517732188-mxcaav].
  - **Peek at 768:** the 900px min-height is split evenly between the two wrapped rows. The sidebar row is ~520px tall with ~300px of nothing under the file list, and the card gets ~280px [blob-1792517819751-ectx8z].
  - **Flush at 768:** the sidebar's `border-right` becomes a stray rule that stops mid-screen [blob-1792518014449-n3lh7m].
  - Main's `padding:0 16px 16px 8px` leaves an 8px left margin and a 16px right margin once the sidebar is gone [blob-1792517724171-suvv6m].
- **Why it matters:**
  - Spec §3.4: the sidebar **collapses** (to »). It never stacks.
  - 768 is within the 720px minimum window.
- **Fix:**
  - Below ~1000px window width, collapse the sidebar to the » state the spec already defines. Problems, Settings and Find show it.
  - If stacking is kept as a deliberate mock convention: `width:100%`, `max-height:40vh; overflow:auto`, `align-content:flex-start`, symmetric `padding-inline:16px`.

### MAJOR 4: the orange data tint is the warn colour
- **Where:** systemic (Main, Peek, Flush, Find, Stress, Rows, LargeFile, Tree, Markdown).
- **What I saw:**
  - **Background is identical.** In every board's token block, `--orange-bg` equals `--warn-bg` byte for byte: dark `#3A2615`, light `#FDEBD3` (e.g. Main.dc.html:14–15).

  - **Where orange shows up:**

    - JSONL type pills "tool" (Rows) and "job" (LargeFile);
    - YAML/JSON numbers (Tree, Rows' expanded tree);
    - the Markdown WARNING callout [blob-1792517896258-ugop09].
- **Why it matters:**
  - §6.2 reserves `warn` for problems.
  - §5.2: "Callouts never use warn."
  - §7.7: problems must surface unambiguously.
  - On the Problems board, the amber `!` rows sit next to orange "To do" pills of the same hue.
- **Fix:**
  - Re-hue one of the two. Move the 6th data tint off orange, e.g. to yellow: dark bg `#33300F` / tx `#E6D27A`, light bg `#FBF3CF` / tx `#6B5600`. Or move `warn` toward amber-red.
  - Re-run the 4.5:1 checks on both tint backgrounds and `surface`.
  - Callouts then use the new hue.
