## 1. Verdict

- **Mostly holds.** All 45 boards render, and every wrapper board shows its theme or variant.
  - Blockers 1 and 4 hold. Blocker 3 holds at 1440 and below.
- **Two claims fail in render:**
  - **M5:** at 768 the Outline rail is still a 232px column. Inside the spec's window range the overlay never triggers.
  - **Indent guides:** still invisible at 1:1.
- **Two regressions came in with the fixes:**
  - Native **light scrollbars inside every dark board** that scrolls.
  - **Main's status-pill column is off-screen at the 1440 design size.** The "content-fitted" column widths are fixed px values that are wider than the content.

## 2. Method

**Version tested**
- The live canvas was **not** the v22 token (`1792520682-fc67`) named in §6:
  - at the start it was `1792521347-e5ab`;
  - at the end it was `1792522897-0cfd`.
- **`e5ab`:** all 46 `project/*` files are byte-identical to the local scratchpad sources (`cmp`, 0 diffs).
- **`0cfd`:** all 46 project file sizes are unchanged, and 8 sampled files (Main, Markdown, Stress, Peek, Tree, Settings, Problems, canvas.json) have the same sha256.
  - Only `artifact-type/app.js` and `app.css` changed. That is a runtime update to the Design type, not a content edit.
- **Conclusion:** what I rendered is the fix-pass source.

**Widths. Two deviations from the brief, both forced by the harness:**
- **The browser pane is 1276×1146.** An emulated viewport wider than the pane does not re-lay-out the cross-origin board iframe: the board stays at about 1277px and the rest is unpainted (blob-1792521630694-xdf1wt, -1792521669864-5qwe87, both discarded).
- **1440×900 → play view "Fit" mode.** Fit lays a PAGE board out at its canvas size, exactly 1440×900, then scales it down.
  - Confirmed by the frame aspect (1.6).
  - Confirmed by the Peek: it measures about 529px, which matches the triage's computed 1440 value.
  - This is a truer 1440×900 than the previous reviewer's Fill view, which gave about 1425×820.
- **1280×800 → 1276×800 Fill.** That's 4px narrower than asked, so slightly harsher.
- **1024×768 and 768×1024:** Fill, as specified.
- **Fixed boards:** Fit at native size.
- **Extra probes:**
  - Main at 1000 / 999 / 990 and at 1024×600;
  - Problems at 1100 / 1060;
  - Markdown at 620 (below the spec minimum), only to exercise the rail overlay.

**What I could not check**
- **Any Fill width above 1276.** So the peek's labelled-actions state (peek ≥ 560px, needs a window of about 1509px or more) was **not checked in render** (B3b).
- **The labels are present in source** (Peek.dc.html:125–126). They are hidden by `@container peek (max-width: 559px)` (Peek.dc.html:26). Because they are hidden at every tested width, the container query is live. The "labels show" state is source only.
- **No DOM access inside the boards.** Board text and structure come from screenshots plus source.
- **Pixel measurements at 1440 and 1276** are from downscaled captures (about 0.535 and 0.627) and are ±2px. 768 is 1:1.

**Overview thumbnails**
- The overview's hatched thumbnails (PeekBack, Stress, Title bars, InteractionLight, and later Markdown) are lazily captured placeholders.
- Every one of those boards renders when opened.

## 5. New regressions

**R1 · MAJOR: native light scrollbars in dark boards (systemic)**
- **Cause:** `overflow:auto` card bodies (the M1 fix) with no `color-scheme: dark` on `.t-dark` (Main.dc.html:16; KIT.md:27; the same helmet is in all 40 PAGE files). Chrome draws white tracks and thumbs.
- **Where:**
  - Main md and csv cards at 1276, 1024 and 768 (blob-1792521622489-3dtgwk, blob-1792521844193-y5c96f);
  - Tree, both cards (blob-1792522157454-p6mlgz);
  - MarkdownDark (blob-1792522097294-zdifln);
  - TranscriptClaude at 768 (blob-1792522237632-qpp0j0);
  - the PeekDark peek grid;
  - the Problems and Stress card rows at 768 (blob-1792522413338-e3pby6).
- **Why it matters:** same class of bug as the white band M1 fixed. Implementers will copy it.

**R2 · MAJOR: the hero grid hides its status pills at the 1440 design size**
- **What:** Main and Flush (and the Peek grid, which shows one letter of each pill).
- **Cause:** the fixed "content-fitted" widths (220 / 140 / 96 / 96) are 60–70px wider than their content (Main.dc.html:184, 193).
- **Before the fix:** status was visible at 1440 (it only wrapped below about 1330).
- **Now:** status needs a horizontal scroll at the design size.

**R3 · MINOR: the breadcrumb root collapses to nothing, leaving an orphan separator**
- PeekBack at 1024 and 768 reads "← ▦ / tasks.csv" (blob-1792522014045-6qfepi, -os0213). Source: Peek.dc.html:114–116, root `min-width:0` with no "…" fallback.
- Stress's README card shows the middle segment as "d." instead of "…" (blob-1792522635231-t2tm6l).

**R4 · MINOR: SettingsDark contradicts itself**
- It renders the dark theme but labels the Light slot "in use".
- `Light theme · in use` is hard-coded at Settings.dc.html:120.

**R5 · MINOR: SettingsParseError's title and content disagree**

**R6 · MINOR: Interaction's pill-row demo clips the focused pill**

**R7 · MINOR (spec): residual "orange" in the brief**
